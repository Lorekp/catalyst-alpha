Our Strategy

Event-driven alpha from behavioral inefficiencies in crypto markets. Three independent edges, one systematic framework.

The Core Thesis

Crypto markets are uniquely prone to behavioral mispricing around structural events. Token unlocks, funding rate extremes, and new token distributions create predictable supply/demand imbalances that persist because most market participants are retail-driven and reactive rather than systematic.

We don't predict markets. We identify events where the statistical distribution of outcomes is skewed in our favor, then position systematically.

1. Token Unlock Events

Behavioral basis: When locked tokens vest and become liquid, early investors and team members often sell — creating a supply shock. Markets consistently underestimate the magnitude of this selling pressure, even when unlock schedules are publicly known.

  • We monitor upcoming unlock events across the ecosystem
  • We filter for events where unlock size is material relative to circulating supply
  • Positions are sized based on event magnitude and historical response patterns
  • Exit criteria are predefined — no discretionary holds

2. Funding Rate Mean-Reversion

Behavioral basis: Perpetual futures funding rates reflect the balance of long vs. short positioning. When funding reaches extremes, it signals overcrowded positioning and statistically reverts. This is a well-documented inefficiency rooted in leverage-driven herd behavior.

  • Continuous monitoring of funding rates across assets
  • Statistical detection of extreme deviations from equilibrium
  • Position entry when rates reach historically significant levels
  • Mean-reversion capture with defined risk parameters

3. Airdrop & TGE Distribution

Behavioral basis: When tokens are airdropped to users or launched via TGE (Token Generation Event), recipients overwhelmingly sell immediately — "free money" has near-zero holding incentive. This creates a predictable post-distribution price decline that the market prices inefficiently at listing.

  • We track upcoming airdrops and new token listings
  • We assess the distribution dynamics (recipient count, vesting, market cap)
  • Short positions capture the post-distribution normalization
  • Strict timing windows prevent overexposure

Academic Foundations

Our strategies draw from established research in behavioral finance and market microstructure:

  • Prospect theory — Asymmetric risk preferences drive sell pressure on "free" tokens
  • Supply shock literature — Predictable supply increases cause temporary dislocations
  • Mean-reversion in leveraged markets — Overcrowded positioning reverts as leverage unwinds
  • AFML framework — Advances in Financial Machine Learning informs our validation pipeline

Risk Management

Every position has predefined risk parameters. We never override systematic rules with discretionary judgment.

  • Position sizing based on event magnitude and volatility
  • Maximum drawdown limits per strategy and portfolio-wide
  • Correlation monitoring to avoid concentrated exposure
  • All strategies run independently — no single point of failure
