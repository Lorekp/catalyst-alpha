Our Methodology

Rigorous quantitative research. Systematic validation. No shortcuts.

Who We Are

Catalyst Alpha is a quantitative research team focused on crypto market microstructure. We specialize in identifying and exploiting event-driven inefficiencies using systematic, rules-based strategies.

Our team combines expertise in quantitative finance, behavioral economics, and crypto-native infrastructure. We build our own tooling, run our own infrastructure, and validate every hypothesis before risking capital.

The AFML Framework

Our research pipeline is built on principles from Advances in Financial Machine Learning by Marcos López de Prado — the gold standard for avoiding overfitting and false discoveries in quantitative finance.

  • Triple barrier labeling — Events are classified by outcome, not arbitrary time windows
  • Combinatorial purged cross-validation — Backtests that prevent lookahead bias
  • Walk-forward analysis — Out-of-sample validation on every strategy
  • Feature importance — We understand why a strategy works, not just that it works

Validation Pipeline

Every strategy passes through a rigorous multi-stage validation before deployment. No exceptions.

  1. Hypothesis formation — Behavioral or structural basis identified. We start with the "why" before looking at data.
  2. Historical analysis — Statistical validation across multiple market regimes and time periods.
  3. Walk-forward testing — Out-of-sample performance on data the model has never seen. Strategies must pass strict Sharpe and drawdown criteria.
  4. Paper trading — Live market execution without real capital. Verifies signal generation, latency, and infrastructure reliability.
  5. Gradual deployment — Capital allocated incrementally. Continuous monitoring against paper trade benchmarks.
