Three Structural Edges

Each strategy exploits a well-documented behavioral bias in crypto markets — events where supply, demand, or incentives create predictable price dislocations.

🔓

Token Unlock Shorts

Large token unlocks create selling pressure as early investors and teams receive liquid tokens. We systematically identify and position ahead of these supply shocks.

⚖

Funding Rate Arbitrage

When perpetual futures funding rates deviate from fair value, mean-reversion is statistically reliable. We capture the spread between extreme funding and equilibrium.

🏁

Airdrop & TGE Shorts

New token launches and airdrops follow a predictable pattern: euphoric listing, rapid distribution by recipients, and price normalization. We position for the decay.
